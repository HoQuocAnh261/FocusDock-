Privacy Policy

FocusDock Desktop Application • Effective Date: September 26, 2026
FocusDock operates 100% Offline. No data ever leaves your device. Zero cloud sync, zero tracking, zero analytics.

1. Overview

FocusDock ("the Application") is a Pomodoro focus timer and ambient sound dock built with a strict privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your focus sessions, goals, reminders, daily stats, and work rhythms are entirely personal and belong exclusively to you.

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusDock handles information when you install and use the application via Microsoft Store or as a Progressive Web Application (PWA) on Windows 11.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because FocusDock runs entirely client-side without any remote backend servers or third-party SDKs, we do not collect:

  • Personal Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials of any kind.
  • Session & Focus Data: All Pomodoro sessions, daily goals, custom session targets, completion streaks, and focus history stay strictly in your device's local storage.
  • Reminder & Schedule Data: Daily reminders, alarm times, and activity names you configure are stored entirely on-device and never sent anywhere.
  • Audio & Sound Files: Any custom ambient sound files you upload (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG) are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. They are never uploaded or transmitted.
  • Telemetry & Analytics: No tracking scripts, analytics beacons, crash reporters, or usage monitoring of any kind.
  • Device Information: No device fingerprints, IP addresses, MAC addresses, or location data.
  • Cookies & Identifiers: The application does not set or read any advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How Your Data is Stored Locally

All of your data — including Pomodoro settings, session goals, daily statistics, completion streaks, reminder schedules, custom sound files, theme preferences, and language settings — is stored exclusively on your local device using two standard browser APIs:

  • localStorage: Stores settings, stats, reminders, and preferences as JSON strings.
  • IndexedDB: Stores custom ambient audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG) you choose to upload into the app.

This data is sandboxed to your device and is never uploaded, synced, or shared with any remote server or third party.

4. Local Server Architecture

When running as a Windows desktop application, FocusDock uses a lightweight local Node.js HTTP server bound exclusively to 127.0.0.1:8081 (localhost). This server:

  • Is not accessible from the internet or any external network — it only serves files to your local browser window.
  • Provides two local API endpoints (/api/startup) that interact only with your own Windows Registry to manage the "Start with Windows" toggle. No data leaves your machine.
  • Makes zero outbound network requests of any kind during operation.

5. Offline Mode & Service Worker

FocusDock is powered by an offline Service Worker that caches all application code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons) on your local hard drive. Once installed, FocusDock operates with zero internet connectivity. All procedural ambient sounds (Brown Noise, Rain, Campfire, Alpha Waves, Pink Noise, White Noise) are generated in real-time using the Web Audio API — no audio files are ever downloaded from the internet.

6. Data Deletion Rights

You have complete ownership and control over all your data at any time:

  • Settings Reset: Open Settings inside the app to reset preferences to defaults.
  • Reminders: Delete individual reminders or all reminders via the Reminders modal.
  • Custom Sounds: Delete custom uploaded audio files directly from the Soundscapes panel.
  • Full Wipe: Uninstalling the application or clearing your browser/app storage removes all data permanently from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

FocusDock does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no social features. It is completely safe for all audiences.

8. Compliance with Microsoft Store Policy

This policy complies with the Microsoft Store Developer Agreement and App Certification Requirements (Section 10.5.1: Personal Information). FocusDock has no capability to collect, transmit, or store personal data on remote servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be included in the next application update submitted to the Microsoft Store. Continued use of FocusDock after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the development team through the official GitHub repository issue tracker or your Microsoft Store developer support link. We aim to respond within 3 business days.
